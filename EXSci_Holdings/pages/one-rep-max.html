Calculation

This is an estimate, not a direct measurement of strength. Accuracy depends on the equation, exercise, technique, and the effort made during the set.

Understanding the Equation

The Epley equation estimates your one-repetition maximum (1RM) from the weight you lifted and the number of repetitions completed.

1RM = Weight × (1 + Repetitions ÷ 30)

Example: If you lift 60 kg for 8 repetitions, the estimated 1RM is 76 kg.

Avoid attempting a true maximum lift without suitable technique, preparation, and appropriate supervision.
